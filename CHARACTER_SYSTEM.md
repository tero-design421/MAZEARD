MAZEARD – Character System

1. Character Roles

Characters have different combat roles:

- Tank: Absorbs damage and protects teammates.
- Ranged: Attacks enemies from long distances.
- Healer: Restores health to teammates.
- Support: Enhances team abilities and provides tactical advantages.
- Fighter: Specializes in direct combat.

2. Unique Character Abilities

Every character has unique abilities that influence battle strategy.

Examples:

- Team Power Boost: Doubles the team's power for one turn, followed by a two-turn cooldown.
- Enemy Conversion: Temporarily turns an enemy into an ally for one turn, changing a 5v5 battle into a 6v4 battle.
- Group Healing: Charges for seven turns, then heals all teammates.
- Long-Range Attack: Allows ranged characters to attack from greater distances.
- One-Time Revival: Revives a defeated teammate once per game.

3. Cooldown System

Special abilities have different cooldown periods depending on their strength.

4. Character Progression

Characters have different ranks and ratings.

The character card's aura color changes according to the character's rank, while the original yellow background and card frame remain unchanged.

5. Team Composition

Players must build balanced teams using different character roles and abilities.

Team composition is an important part of tactical combat.

6. Design Philosophy

Every character should feel unique.

Character abilities should create meaningful strategic decisions rather than simply increasing attack or defense stats.
