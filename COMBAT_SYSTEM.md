MAZEARD – Combat System

1. Battle Format

- Initial battle format: 5 vs 5.
- Turn-based tactical combat.
- Battles take place on an isometric grid.

2. Movement

- Characters move across the tactical battlefield.
- Positioning and distance affect combat decisions.

3. Combat Mechanics

- Characters use basic attacks and unique abilities.
- Each ability has specific effects and cooldowns.
- Team composition influences battle strategy.

4. Character Roles

- Tanks absorb damage.
- Ranged characters attack from longer distances.
- Healers restore health.
- Support characters enhance teammates.
- Fighters specialize in direct combat.

5. Special Abilities

Combat may include:

- Temporary team power boosts.
- Temporary enemy conversion.
- Group healing.
- Long-range attacks.
- One-time character revival.

6. Tactical Strategy

Players must consider:

- Character positioning.
- Ability timing.
- Cooldown management.
- Team synergy.
- Enemy threats.

7. Combat Design Goal

Every battle should reward strategic thinking, smart positioning, and effective use of character abilities.
