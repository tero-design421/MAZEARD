MAZEARD – Expedition System

1. Expedition Overview

Expeditions are maze-based adventures where players explore dangerous environments and discover secrets.

2. Maze Structure

- The expedition uses a 10x10 grid.
- Each tile represents a possible location, encounter, or event.
- The maze contains different paths and environmental challenges.

3. Movement System

- A dice determines movement.
- A compass determines a random direction when activated.
- Movement follows the results of the dice and compass.

4. Event Cards

Players encounter event cards during exploration.

Events may include:

- Environmental challenges.
- Puzzles.
- Encounters.
- Rewards.
- Unexpected consequences.

5. Failed Challenges

- If a player fails a challenge, they return to their original tile.
- The attempted movement is canceled.

6. Time Reversal

Players have a limited number of attempts to reverse time during an expedition.

The initial design allows two or three attempts per mission, with the choice to retry or withdraw.

7. Expedition Goal

Explore the maze, overcome challenges, manage limited retries, and reach the expedition objective.
