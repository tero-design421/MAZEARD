# MAZEARD — Game Design Document

## 1. Game Vision
MAZEARD is an indie fantasy-futuristic adventure and tactical RPG.

## 2. World
A magical future world combining forests, ancient ruins, advanced technology, cyberpunk environments, and hybrid regions.

## 3. Core Gameplay
- Explore villages in third-person view.
- Meet NPCs and discover recruitable characters.
- Learn each character's story and abilities.
- Build a team for expeditions.
- Fight tactical turn-based battles on an isometric grid.

## 4. Combat
- Tactical grid-based combat.
- Initial target: 5 vs 5 battles.
- Unique character abilities and cooldowns.
- Roles include tanks, ranged fighters, healers, and support characters.

## 5. Character Cards
Preserve the original purple and gold frame, yellow inner background, and cream nameplate. Only the aura behind the character portrait changes color according to character rank.

## 6. Character Abilities
Examples include team power boosts, temporary enemy conversion, group healing, long-range attacks, and one-time revival.

## 7. Exploration
The village uses a behind-the-back third-person camera. Players explore, talk to NPCs, receive recommendations, and choose recruits.

## 8. Expedition
The expedition includes maze exploration, environmental challenges, encounters, and discoveries.

## 9. Visual Identity
The first region emphasizes forests and nature with subtle futuristic elements. Other regions may feature cyberpunk technology or a blend of magic, nature, and technology.

## 10. Development Goal
Build MAZEARD incrementally using free tools, mobile-friendly workflows, and no paid subscriptions. Start with a small playable prototype before expanding.
