MAZEARD – Village System

1. Village Exploration

- The village is explored using a third-person, behind-the-back camera.
- Players can freely walk around and explore the environment.
- Players can interact with NPCs and discover hidden stories.

2. NPC Interactions

- NPCs provide information about the world.
- Some NPCs recommend potential recruits.
- Players can discover new characters through conversations.

3. Character Recruitment

- Talk to NPCs to receive character recommendations.
- Find and meet recommended characters.
- Learn their stories, abilities, strengths, and weaknesses.
- Decide whether to recruit them.

4. Player Freedom

- Players choose which characters to recruit.
- Recruitment should involve exploration and meaningful conversations rather than a simple selection menu.

5. Visual Direction

- Natural fantasy villages.
- Ancient architecture.
- Subtle futuristic elements.
- Different visual identities depending on the region.

6. Design Goal

Make villages feel alive, encourage exploration, and make discovering new characters an enjoyable experience.
